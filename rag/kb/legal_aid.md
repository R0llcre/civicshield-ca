# Legal Aid (CA) — Finding Help Safely

This knowledge base file is for **general information only** and is **not legal advice**.

## 1) What “legal aid” means and what to expect

**What it is**
- Legal aid organizations may provide free or low-cost help, clinics, referrals, and self-help resources.
- Many organizations use intake screening and may have waitlists; availability varies by county, language, and case type.

**What to do next**
- Start with organizations that serve your county and preferred language.
- If you have a deadline (for example, a hearing date), mention it during intake and ask about timelines.

**Sources**
- https://www.lawhelpca.org/

## 2) How to choose where to apply (county/language/service)

**What it is**
- Some services are county-limited; some are statewide.
- Many organizations specialize (asylum, family-based, removal defense, DACA, etc.).

**What to do next**
- Filter by county and language first, then by service category.
- Prefer programs that explicitly list immigration services rather than generic “legal help.”
- If you are unsure, ask for a referral to a qualified attorney or accredited representative.

**Sources**
- https://www.immigrationadvocates.org/

## 3) What “intake status” usually means (open / waitlist / closed)

**What it is**
- **Open**: the organization is currently accepting new intakes (still may have delays).
- **Waitlist**: they may accept your information but cannot take immediate action.
- **Closed**: they are not accepting new cases right now.

**What to do next**
- If status is waitlist/closed, try an alternative organization or ask for a referral list.
- Keep a short log of who you contacted and when (for your own tracking).

**Sources**
- https://www.lawhelpca.org/

## 4) What to prepare before contacting legal aid

**What it is**
- Being prepared helps you use short intake windows effectively.

**What to do next**
- Prepare:
  - key dates (deadlines, hearing dates)
  - basic case type (in plain language)
  - documents you already have (do not send sensitive IDs unless requested through an official channel)
- If you need to share files, prefer secure, official methods; do not upload to unknown links.

**Sources**
- https://www.justice.gov/
